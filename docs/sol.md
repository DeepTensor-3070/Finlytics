# Core Features
1. Financial Document Intelligence
    Upload and process financial statements and business documents.
    Extract key financial metrics and relevant information.
    Identify missing, inconsistent, or conflicting financial records.

2. Financial Risk Detection
    Assess repayment capacity and financial stability.
    Detect potential warning signs in financial records.
    Identify unpaid invoices, unusual financial patterns, and possible risk indicators.

3. AI-Powered External Investigation
    Investigate companies using external web sources.
    Find adverse news, legal developments, business concerns, and other relevant risk signals.
    Select investigation tools based on the identified risk.

4. Evidence Verification and Entity Matching
    Verify that information refers to the correct company.
    Cross-check findings across sources.
    Identify contradictory claims and distinguish verified facts from uncertain information.

5. Risk-Driven Agent Workflow
    Coordinate specialized AI agents across the investigation process.
    Choose relevant tools and research steps dynamically.
    Connect internal financial findings with external evidence.

6. Explainable Risk Reports
    Generate structured financial due-diligence reports.
    Link risk conclusions to supporting evidence and source citations.
    Explain why a risk was identified and its potential implications.

7. Auditability and Human Oversight
    Maintain traceability between findings, evidence, and conclusions.
    Flag uncertain or conflicting findings for review.
    Allow human reviewers to validate important conclusions before making decisions.

# End to End Flow

```
Understand (Company and document intake) -> Analyze(Financial metrics and risk detection) -> Investigate (Risk-triggered external research) -> Verify (Source validation and company identity checks) -> Explain (Evidence-backed report and human review)
```
