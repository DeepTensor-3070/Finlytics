#Finlytics is an AI-powered financial due diligence system designed to help banks, NBFCs and fintech lenders assess the financial health and credit risk of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).

#What exactly is financial due diligence? Financial due diligence is the process of examining a business's financial position and checking whether the information it presents is reliable enough to support a decision.

*For a lender considering an MSME loan, this can involve checking:

How much revenue the business generates.
Whether that revenue is growing or declining.
Whether the business actually receives cash from its customers.
Whether it has enough cash to repay its debt.
Whether its liabilities and outstanding loans are manageable.
Whether its documents contain inconsistencies or unusual transactions.
Whether events outside the company's accounts threaten its future income.
::For example, suppose a manufacturing company reports revenue growth of 32%. At first glance, this may look positive. However, its operating cash flow has declined by 18%, while its receivables have increased by 47%.
